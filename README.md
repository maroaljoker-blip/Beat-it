# Beat-it
 is a game focused on competing against a boss by bouncing a ball back and forth while trying to get the ball past your opponent.

Gameplay

You play as the paddle on the left side, while the boss controls the paddle on the right side.

The ball bounces between you and the opponet. The angle of the ball changes depending on where it hits the paddle, so you can control the direction of the ball by hitting it near the top, middle, or bottom.



The ball also becomes faster as the game continues, making it harder to keep up.

If the ball goes outside the play area, it resets to the center and its speed returns to the starting speed.

Controls

Up Arrow or W – Move Up
Down Arrow or S – Move Down

Features



The ball has different bounce angles depending on where it hits the paddle.

The ball becomes faster every few seconds.

The ball resets when it leaves the play area.

Three-heart health system.

Boss reaction mistakes to make the game more challenging and fair.

Smooth paddle movement.
